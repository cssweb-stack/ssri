SSRI: Research and Grant Services

The Social Science Research Institute (SSRI), the sponsored research division of the College of Social Sciences at the University of Hawaiʻi at Mānoa, conducts and supports interdisciplinary, applied research addressing critical social, behavioral, economic, and environmental issues in Hawaiʻi and the Asia Pacific region. SSRI also provides grant development and administrative support for extramural research and training grants, including pre- and post-award services.

Interdisciplinary Research

SSRI conducts and supports interdisciplinary, applied research that addresses critical social, behavioral, economic, and environmental problems, primarily in Hawaiʻi and the Asia Pacific region.

Extramural Grant Administration

SSRI provides a comprehensive array of extramural support services to faculty, from identifying funding sources to proposal development and initiating travel requests. This “life cycle” approach to grant-making activities includes grant development services, workshops and training, research services, compliance review, fiscal and administrative support, and assisting principal investigators in managing awards.
